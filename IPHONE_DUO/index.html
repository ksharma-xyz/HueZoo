    iPhone Duo and resizable windows¶

    Huezoo sizes its layout to the window, never to the device. iPhone Duo (closed and open), iPad, landscape phones and resizable windows all go through the same code.

    Displays¶

    Display Pixels Points (3 px/pt) Notes
    Duo outer (closed) 1398 x 2034 466 x 678 Status bar and camera sit in a column on the right edge
    Duo inner (open), portrait 2007 x 2853 669 x 951
    Duo inner (open), landscape 2853 x 2007 951 x 669
    Resize mode arbitrary about 375 to 1165 wide

    Fold or hinge aware layout is out of scope. The app treats each display as a plain window.

    Toolchain¶

    The app must be built with Xcode 27.1 or later (iOS 27.1 SDK), otherwise iOS letterboxes it on Duo. Built with Xcode 26 the outer display shows a black strip where the status column is; built with 27.x the app draws under it, which is why the inset rules below matter. CI uses the xcode-27 runner with an exact 27.1 pin (build-ios.yml, distribute-testflight.yml). Do not set UIRequiresFullScreen; there is none today.

    Apple requires Duo screenshots from April 2027 for apps built with the iOS 27.1 SDK or later. Submitting them adds the optimized for Duo badge.

    Inset rules¶

    All helpers live in ui/theme/CornerSafeInsets.kt.

    • Top: keep statusBarsPadding() (or the status bar spacer in HuezooTopBar) and add cornerClearanceTop() after it. It is zero on phones and 16dp minus the status bar on the Duo outer display, where the top inset is about 0 and nothing else moves the title out of the rounded corner. Do not replace statusBarsPadding() with windowInsetsPadding(safeDrawing) on top: where a parent already consumed the status bars the gap doubles.
    • Sides: Modifier.safeHorizontalPadding() is safeDrawing.only(Horizontal). Compose on iOS reports displayCutout on the top edge only, so displayCutout padding is zero on the Duo outer display. Use safeDrawing for the sides.
    • Bottom: navigationBarsPadding() as before (home indicator).
    • Backgrounds stay full-bleed: apply the background before the padding. AmbientGlowBackground does this for you: glows are full-bleed, its content is inset. HuezooTopBar insets its button row; HuezooBottomSheet and HuezooAlertDialog inset their content. New screens built on AmbientGlowBackground need nothing extra for the sides.
    • Pinned banner ads use safeHorizontalPadding() too.

    Window size rules¶

    Window classes come from the window width in dp (windowClassFor, read through BoxWithConstraints), never from the device. Helpers live in ui/theme/WindowClass.kt.

    Class Width Layout
    Compact below 600dp The phone layout, unchanged. Swatches are 300dp.
    Medium 600 to 840dp One column capped at MaxContentWidth (640dp). Game swatches scale.
    Expanded 840dp and wider Multi-pane layouts.
    • contentMaxWidth() caps a screen's main column at MaxContentWidth (640dp) and fills narrower windows. Place it with Alignment.TopCenter so it centres on wide windows.
    • PageColumn is the page column for game screens: 640dp on Compact and Medium, and on Expanded ExpandedMaxWidth (1200dp) or the expandedWidth the screen passes. Threshold and Daily pass GamePageWidth (976dp = 360dp HUD pane + 24dp gap + 560dp swatch + 16dp insets), so the top bar's edges are the panes' outer edges with no leftover side space. Color Memory uses the full 1200dp.
    • Dialog cards are capped at 440dp.
    • Panes are inset 16dp on the sides, the same inset as the top bar's buttons, and sit 24dp apart (PaneGap). Panes in a row start on the same line; the game panes also end on the same line.
    • Secondary elements are sized from the primary one (swatch size, card unit) and never stretched to fill leftover space: extra space becomes margin.

    Home¶

    • Compact and Medium: one scrolling column, as before.
    • Expanded: top bar across the page, then two top-aligned panes. "You" (width 5/12 of the page, clamped 320 to 440dp): callsign, gems, streak, personal best, rank progress, leaderboard. "Play": the Threshold hero, then Daily and Color Memory side by side with equal heights, then "What is ΔE?". When the Play pane is narrower than 600dp (windows around 950dp) Daily and Color Memory stack instead so their text is not cut. The page scrolls when the window is short.

    Threshold and Daily¶

    • RadialSwatchLayout(size = ...) scales the whole geometry (tiles, gap, container) from the 300dp design size. Compact keeps 300dp.
    • Medium, and Expanded portrait: stacked column, swatches scale to min(width - 48dp, height - header, 520dp) (never below 300dp), and the group is centred vertically.
    • Expanded landscape (width greater than height, room for at least a MinPaneSwatchSize 200dp swatch): a supporting pane. The left pane (360dp) has one left edge. Threshold: title with lives and tries directly under it at the top, the ΔE badge, caption and session best in the middle, feedback at the bottom. Daily: title and date, round chip with its dots, feedback. The swatch (up to 560dp, min(pane width, window height - banner)) sits in a row exactly as tall as the swatch, so the pane's top and bottom are the swatch's top and bottom.
    • Phone held sideways (about 914x411dp) is Expanded by width: Threshold and Daily use this layout with a small swatch; Home scrolls its two columns.

    Color Memory¶

    • Expanded (and tall enough): a fixed 360dp controls pane (HUD at the top, headline and readout, SAME/DIFFERENT pinned to the bottom) beside the two chambers. Chamber height is capped at 1.4 times their width (it shrinks, never stretches), the row is centred vertically, and the controls pane is exactly as tall as the chambers so tops and bottoms coincide.
    • Below 560dp window height, or when the Expanded row would be under 320dp tall (phone held sideways): HUD, headline, readout and buttons on the left, chambers on the right (nothing scrolls away during the timed phases).
    • Otherwise they stack and the chambers flex to fill the space.

    Other screens¶

    • Every other screen scrolls vertically, so short windows (phone held sideways) scroll rather than clip. Threshold and Daily add BannerReserve bottom padding so the swatches can scroll clear of the pinned banner.
    • Settings, Leaderboard and the other reading screens keep a single 640dp column.

    Testing on the Duo simulator¶

    xcodebuild -project iosApp/iosApp.xcodeproj -scheme iosApp -configuration Debug \
  -destination 'id=<duo-udid>' -derivedDataPath <scratch>/dd CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO build
xcrun simctl install <duo-udid> <scratch>/dd/Build/Products/Debug-iphonesimulator/Huezoo.app
xcrun simctl launch <duo-udid> xyz.ksharma.huezoo
xcrun simctl io <duo-udid> screenshot --display=1 outer.png   # outer, 1398x2034
xcrun simctl io <duo-udid> screenshot --display=3 inner.png   # inner, 2007x2853; black unless open
    • Maestro drives the outer display (maestro --device <udid> test flow.yaml). Percent points work; the settings gear is exposed as the text ⚙, the back button only by point.
    • Settings has debug toggles (HIDE ADS, SWITCH TO PAID) that give clean screenshots and reach the leaderboard without a purchase. Debug builds only.
    • To test landscape without rotating the simulator, pass INFOPLIST_KEY_UISupportedInterfaceOrientations_iPhone=UIInterfaceOrientationLandscapeRight (and _iPad) to xcodebuild for a throwaway build.
    • A leftover ATT dialog from another app can sit over the screen: tap "Ask App Not to Track".

    Not covered¶

    • Fold or hinge aware layout, and splitting content across the two inner halves.
    • The inner display could not be lit from the command line; it is covered by wide-window layouts (iPad, landscape) rather than on the Duo itself.
